CORE SERVICES

HONESTY.INTEGRITY.QUALITY

MPI Oilfield Inc operates throughout western Canada with complete service uniformity provided to our energy client base. MPI specializes in thermal pipelining, facility construction, turnarounds, plant and field maintenance.

Established in 2006, MPI Oilfield delivers world-class fabrication, installation, construction and facility maintenance services for thermal/conventional oil and natural gas producers. For over a decade, MPI has established innovative methodologies for industry specific construction requirements to safely deliver seamless installation and project completion for our clients.

MPI Oilfield is a field to facility thermal service provider and constructor— a “Single Thermal Source”.

PAD AND PIPELINE CONSTRUCTION​

MPI Oilfield has the experience and expertise to complete a broad range of sustainable gathering lines projects—both below and above ground—with a special emphasis on above-ground sustainable gathering lines systems integral to steam assisted gravity drainage (SAGD) projects in western Canada’s Bitumen Belt, between Fort McMurray, AB and North Battleford, SK. These systems include steam supply lines (built to CSA Z662 specifications); and all emulsion, product and fuel-gas supply lines required for safe and efficient in situ bitumen production.

MPI Oilfield holds all of the necessary ABSA & TSASK certifications for the fabrication, installation and maintenance of above-ground steam gathering lines in Alberta | Saskatchewan. In specific: High carbon alloys. Our highly skilled welders, quality control staff and inspectors have safely and efficiently completed installations in the western Canadian thermal SAGD region from Fort McMurray, AB to North Battleford, SK.

Facility Maintenance & Turnaround

MPI Oilfield specializes in facility construction, with full ABSA & TSASK certifications to build or repair—in the shop or in the field—boilers, pressure vessels and pressure piping. From large-scale SAGD facilities to gas metering stations and oil batteries, we have fully certified crews ready to meet your needs.

Turnaround / Outages Services
MPI Oilfield offers a full suite of plant-turnaround and field-maintenance services; facility modifications; and sustainable pipeline-maintenance and repair services, including integrity digs and inspections of buried pipelines. Our ultimate goal is to complete these maintenance and repair projects quickly, safely and professionally, minimizing production downtime.

Field Maintenance & Integrity

With certified CWB welders on staff and a wealth of in-house fabrication expertise, MPI is well positioned to provide a variety of field maintenance services and ILI (in-line inspections). In addition to in-shop fabrication, we provide transportation services to move pumpjacks and a variety of vessel units to the field for installation. We also provide pumpjack maintenance services for single wells or multi-well pads. And in the event of a field incident, we offer a full suite of emergency-shutdown services and response plans.

Integrity pipeline exposure and repair programs are essential to healthy and safe field function. MPI is an industry leader of safe line exposure and critical live line repair work.

FABRICATION

MPI Oilfield operates 80,000 square feet of heated, indoor fabrication space in Bonnyville, in the heart of Alberta’s in situ bitumen production region. Our fully certified staff of RMD | STT® (surface tension transfer) welders, pressure welders, structural welders, pipefitters, equipment operators and labourers have the expertise and experience to meet a variety of original client fabrication needs. MPI Oilfield staff can also manage costs effectively and safely while providing required repairs to structures, pipes and tanks.

Lifting, Rigging & Heavy Transportation

At MPI Oilfield, we are able to transport loads of varying size and content. Using our modern fleet of trucks, tractors and trailers, we move small and large loads, including heavy equipment. Our ability to move material for thermal clients and for ourselves creates added value and complements our menu of related service offerings. Our oilfield hauling operation includes RT/ Crawler cranes and a full fleet of boom trucks (ranging from 1.5 tons to 45 tons) and winch tractors (up to 130 tons) all complete with a variety of trailer setups.

Specialty Services

POST WELD HEAT TREATMENT | HYDRAULIC TORQUING
MPI Oilfield offers a full range of heat-treatment services for pipes, valves, flanges and connections. This includes heat treating, stress relieving, cold pipe cutting and bevelling, cold taps, and pipe coring. For existing facility installs, we offer hydro testing and flange management, hydraulic torquing and bolting services, and on-site machining.

PUMPJACK SERVICES
With certified CWB welders on staff and a wealth of in-house fabrication expertise, MPI is well positioned to provide a variety of pumpjack services. In addition to in-shop fabrication, we provide transportation services to move pumpjack units to the field for installation. We also provide pumpjack maintenance services for single wells or multi-well pads. And in the event of a field upset, we offer a full suite of emergency-shutdown services and procedures.

SCAFFOLDING / INSULATING
MPI Oilfield provides full-scale scaffolding and insulating services for Maintenance, Construction and Capital Project work scopes.

SAFETY & Environment
